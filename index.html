LOOKBOOK — 2023

INDULGE PLAYAS CLUB is a Manila-born clothing brand, running since 2023 — founded by a small circle of friends who wanted pieces they couldn't find in local stores: clean designs, heavyweight fabric, and fits that hold up from the morning commute to the late-night gig. The OG logo tee with its silver stitch marked the beginning, and that same club mark still leads every drop.

The brand grew out of the local scene — the music, the courts, the streets — and it still moves that way. Every drop gets announced on our Facebook page first, reserved by message, then released in numbered batches: meetup around the metro or shipped anywhere in the country — priced by location, one flat rate in Metro Manila and zone rates beyond. That model keeps the circle small and the quality up — pieces we actually wear, tested where we actually live.

Every piece we release carries the club motto — strictly for playas only. It sets the standard for who we build for: people who play hard, work harder, and look right doing both. The circle stays small on purpose, and every new member earns the spot.

01 Small Batches Numbered runs, announced on socials. When a piece sells out, it stays sold out.
02 Local First Designed and printed in the Philippines — by us, for the streets here.
03 Heavy Fabric Thick cotton and sturdy builds that survive the wash, the heat and the grind.
04 Shipping By Location Metro Manila rides one flat rate; outside the metro, shipping is priced by zone — the exact rate shows at checkout before you confirm.

KEEPING HEAVY COTTON HEAVY

  • Wash cold, inside out, with like colors.
  • Hang dry — the dryer is the enemy of heavy cotton.
  • No bleach, no ironing straight on prints or stitch.
  • Fold, don't hang, the knits.

FAQ

Asked all
the time.

When does the first drop land?

The first collection is in the final run right now. The date, the pieces and the prices get announced on our Facebook and Instagram pages first — follow the club and turn on notifications so you're early, not late.

How do I reserve a piece?

Add it to your bag here on the site and check out — or message the club on Facebook or Instagram with the piece name and size. First come, first serve — when a batch is claimed, it's claimed.

How do I pay?

Online orders pay through IPC Pay (QR) at checkout — scan, send, submit your proof of payment, and the club verifies every payment by hand before anything ships. Cards and e-wallet checkouts are coming soon — for now the QR is the only live method. Meetup orders can also be settled with GCash or cash — no hidden fees, ever.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is priced by location: one flat rate in Metro Manila, zone rates for the rest of the country — the exact amount shows in the bag and at checkout before you confirm. Metro Manila usually takes a few days; provinces a little longer.

What if my size sells out?

Pieces run in numbered batches — a sold-out size stays sold out until a restock. Restock news posts on Facebook and Instagram first, and you can message us to be noted for the next run of your size.

How should I wash my pieces?

Cold water, inside out, hang dry. Heavy cotton rewards gentle handling — do that and the prints and stitch stay crisp for years.

Do you do collabs or customs?

Open to the right ones. Send your proposal through the message box on the contact page or DM the club on Instagram with references and quantities — serious inquiries get answers.

How do I know a piece is legit?

Everything sells through this site and the official Facebook and Instagram pages only — no third-party sellers, no reseller drops. If it didn't come from the club, it's not the club.

THE PAPERWORK

Terms &
conditions.

LAST UPDATED — NOVEMBER 2026

THE SHORT VERSION: buy through the shop, pay with IPC Pay (QR), the club verifies every payment by hand, and your order ships anywhere in the Philippines — shipping priced by location. Pre-orders are produced in their window and ship when the run lands. Every verified purchase levels you up — ROOKIE, HOMIE, OG, VIP — with perks at each level. Be straight with us and we'll be straight with you.

01 — ORDERS & PRE-ORDERS

Every piece is released in numbered batches — first come, first serve, no reseller priority and no backdoor list. When a batch sells out it stays sold out until a restock is announced on the club's Facebook and Instagram pages.

Pieces marked PRE-ORDER are produced after the pre-order window closes. The listed ship window for a pre-order piece is an estimate, not a promise of an exact date — you'll get the updated timeline from the club the moment it moves.

  • An order is only locked in once it has a reference number (IPC-XXXXXXXX — eight random characters, so nobody can guess another member's order) on your account.
  • Orders unpaid after 48 hours can be released back to the batch.
  • Prices are in Philippine pesos (PHP); shipping is priced by location and shown at checkout before you confirm.

02 — PRICES & PAYMENT (QR ONLY FOR NOW)

Online orders are paid through IPC Pay — the club's own QR checkout. Scan the QR, send the EXACT order total, then submit the reference number from your e-wallet receipt on the payment screen. Meetup orders can be settled with GCash or cash in person.

  • QR payments are the only live method right now. Card payments and e-wallet checkouts are marked COMING SOON and cannot be used yet.
  • The total is locked in writing the moment the order is placed — no surprise fees, ever.
  • Always scan the club's official QR from the IPC Pay screen — send the exact order total and wait for manual payment verification.

03 — PAYMENT VERIFICATION

Every payment is checked by a human before anything ships. Submitting your reference number and screenshot moves your payment to UNDER VERIFICATION — it never marks itself PAID. A club admin verifies it against the club records and marks it PAID or FAILED from the dashboard.

  • If a payment is marked FAILED, that payment reference is closed. Check your transaction details and start a fresh checkout from the items returned to your bag. Two payment rejections within ten minutes temporarily pause new payment requests for five minutes; contact the club if you believe a rejection was made in error.
  • Every verification decision is recorded with the admin who made it and when.

04 — SHIPPING

Shipping is priced by location — one flat rate in Metro Manila, zone rates everywhere else — and the exact rate shows at checkout before anything goes out. Once payment is verified, the order is packed and handed to a trusted local courier.

  • Metro Manila usually lands within a few days; provinces take a little longer — the exact timing depends on your location and the courier.
  • If the courier doesn't reach your address, the club messages you first to sort a Metro Manila meetup or another drop-off — nothing ships blind and nothing is charged without a heads-up.
  • Double-check your delivery details before placing the order — the club ships to the address on the order.

05 — MEETUPS

Meetups run around Metro Manila. Spots and schedules get confirmed when you reserve — we don't hand out locations in advance. The total is confirmed in writing before you head out.

06 — EXCHANGES & SIZE SWAPS

  • Size swaps are easy before anything ships — message the club and we'll sort it.
  • Got a factory defect? Message the club within 7 days of receiving the piece with a photo — we exchange it.
  • Everything else: pieces verified as delivered to the address on the order are final sale. Heavy cotton is washed cold, inside out — see the care notes on the FAQ page.

07 — ACCOUNTS

  • One account per person — keep your login and password to yourself.
  • Your mobile number is your club ID: one mobile number links to one IPC account, and you can log in with your email or your mobile.
  • Give accurate details when ordering; the club uses them to fulfill your order and nothing else.
  • Locked out? DM @INDULGEPLAYASCLUB on Instagram and the club sorts it manually.

08 — PRIVACY

The club collects only what an order, a ticket or a reply needs: your name, contact details and delivery address. Nothing is sold, shared or used for anything beyond running the shop. The full privacy policy — what we keep, how long, and how to ask for changes — lives one tap away. Want your data deleted? Message the club through the contact form; records the club needs to keep (orders, payments and their verification trail) stay, everything else goes.

09 — MEMBERSHIP LEVELS

Every verified purchase counts toward your club level: ROOKIE from day one, HOMIE at ₱2,500 lifetime paid, OG at ₱7,500 and VIP at ₱15,000. Levels are computed from payments the club has verified — never from orders still pending.

  • Perks stack — each level keeps everything the one below it earns.
  • HOMIE — priority payment verification and first dibs on restocks.
  • OG — 24-hour early access to drops and OG-only voucher codes.
  • VIP — 48-hour first access to every drop, VIP-only pieces, a birthday voucher and size-holds via DM.
  • Perks are the club's goodwill to members. They can be adjusted as the club grows, and abuse — fake proofs, chargebacks — can cost a level.

10 — CHANGES

These terms can be updated as the club grows — the version date at the top always shows what's live. Placing an order means you're good with the terms as written here, plain and simple.

THE PAPERWORK / YOUR DATA

Privacy
policy.

LAST UPDATED — NOVEMBER 2026 · INDULGE PLAYAS CLUB

YOUR DETAILS BELONG TO YOU. IPC uses the information needed to run the store, process orders, verify payments, deliver purchases and respond to messages. We do not sell customer personal information.

01 — INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you create an account, contact the club, open a support ticket or place an order, we may collect your name, email address, mobile number, account credentials, delivery address, order details, message and ticket content, payment reference number and any payment screenshot you choose to submit.

02 — HOW WE USE IT

  • Create and secure your account and prevent misuse of member offers.
  • Confirm orders, calculate discounts, verify payment references and provide customer support.
  • Prepare deliveries and share only the necessary delivery details with the courier or fulfillment provider.
  • Show order, payment and support-ticket updates inside your account — replies and status changes appear on your account page with a NEW REPLY badge. The club does not send marketing email or SMS; there is no mailing list.
  • Protect the store against fraud, duplicate voucher claims and unauthorized activity.

03 — PAYMENT INFORMATION

Online checkout currently uses IPC Pay — the club's own QR checkout. You scan the club's official QR, send the exact order total from your own e-wallet, and submit the reference number and screenshot on the payment screen. IPC stores the order amount, payment reference and submitted proof for manual verification and order records — every payment is checked by a human before anything ships. Never send your wallet password, PIN, one-time password or full card credentials to IPC.

04 — SERVICE PROVIDERS & STORAGE

IPC uses third-party services to host the website and backend and to store account, order and payment-verification records. Information may be processed by those providers only as needed to operate these services. Notifications ride inside the site itself — your account page and your tickets — not through an email or SMS provider. We do not make customer lists available for sale or unrelated advertising.

05 — RETENTION & SECURITY

We keep account, order and payment-verification records for as long as needed to operate the store, resolve disputes, maintain security and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Passwords are stored as secure password hashes, not readable text. No online service can promise absolute security, so keep your login credentials private.

06 — YOUR CHOICES & REQUESTS

You may ask IPC to access, correct or request deletion of personal information, subject to records we are required or reasonably need to retain. Contact the club through the official contact form and include enough detail for us to locate your account or order. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

07 — COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE

The storefront uses browser storage for cart contents, sign-in session details and saved checkout convenience fields. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or remove locally saved cart information. Essential account and order records are stored on the club's backend.

08 — POLICY UPDATES

This policy may be updated as the store and its services change. The date above identifies the latest published version.
